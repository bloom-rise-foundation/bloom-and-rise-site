## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is a tokonoma alcove: one thing placed with patience, charged emptiness around it. It refuses the crowded NGO scroll of equal tiles; space does the speaking, and the honest status is shown as stages of a stem.
OWN-WORLD: plaster cream ground with fine grain, hairline rules and square-cornered frames (2 to 4px radius), a wine lacquer shelf under the bloom, wine and denim rooms, petal/rose only for blooms and accents, iris-denim for links and active state. Playfair at light-to-medium weight with generous measure, small Montserrat in wide-tracked caps for controls; rectangular buttons with an arrow.
STORY: Visitors see candour first (a calm, unhurried page), understand three ways in (learn, join, partner) within the first viewport, and trust the foundation because its status is stated plainly as stages: seed, bud, open.

## Translations and decisions
Catalog palette (plaster, pine, iris blue, bronze) maps onto the brand tokens: plaster = Logo Cream, pine/bronze = Mulberry/Wine, iris = Denim, bloom = Petal/Old Rose. Japanese vertical lettering is not used. The top bar stays (no left rail) so the pinned scroll stories keep their geometry.
